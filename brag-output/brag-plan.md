# Gossip — launch video plan

**What it is:** a free, open-source Mac ↔ Android continuity app — notifications, clipboard, DND, media, mirroring and Universal Control between a Mac and Android phones/tablets. No account, local-first.
**For:** people with a Mac and an Android phone who are jealous of Apple Continuity.
**Sets it apart:** Continuity-level features with no iPhone, no account, no cloud.
**Angle / hook:** "Apple Continuity. Without the iPhone." — then the devices literally start gossiping.
**Punchline:** "And 14 more things your devices now gossip about." → "No account. No cloud. Free and open source."
**Tone:** default — punchy, clean, soft transitions.
**Visual identity:** site dark palette (#101012 bg, #f5f5f7 text, #b0b0b6 muted), accent #0a84ff / icon blue; system font (SF Pro) as the site uses; the real app icon, the real Mac menu-bar dropdown screenshot, the real Android Features screen.
**Share caption:** see share-copy.txt.
**Privacy:** the notification sender ("Maya") and message are fictional stand-ins. Device name "SM-S711B" is a phone model from the public README screenshot.

## Storyboard (21.5s, 30fps, 1920×1080)

| # | Time | Scene |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–3.0 | **Hook.** A Mac and an Android phone slide in, far apart. "Apple Continuity." then "Without the iPhone." A link draws between them at the end. |
| 2 | 3.0–7.0 | **Reveal.** Icon builds node by node, edges draw, blue tile fills. "Gossip" + "Your Mac and your Android devices, working as one." |
| 3 | 7.0–11.5 | **Notifications.** Mac desktop + phone. Real Gossip menu drops from the menu bar (Connected · SM-S711B). A message lands on the phone, flies to the Mac as a banner, reply typed inline, sent; phone confirms. Caption: "Phone notifications on your Mac. Reply inline." |
| 4 | 11.5–15.0 | **Universal Control.** The Mac cursor slides off the right edge and continues onto the phone (real Features screen), clicks a toggle on. Caption: "Push your cursor off the Mac. Keep going on Android." |
| 5 | 15.0–18.0 | **The rest.** The real feature names cascade in as chips. "And 14 more things your devices now gossip about." |
| 6 | 18.0–21.5 | **Outro.** Icon + Gossip. "No account. No cloud. Free and open source." gossip.vmd1.dev |

**Sound:** 120 bpm, A minor → F → C → G pad + soft pluck arpeggio; chime for the notification in-key, soft ticks for typing, a click for the toggle, airy swells for scene changes, a final resolving chord.
